# Series — what the names mean and which application each one serves

Read with `STATE.md`. The meanings live in the Hub: each theme's notes (written 2026-11-27 with Pem's OK) and the idea
« Testament grec — séries au lexique de Strong ». This file says which application uses which series. Verses and
Strong's numbers were written from memory: **check them before quoting in a dossier.**

## The system
- Pem names series after words of the Greek New Testament, with their **Strong's number** (James Strong, 1890:
  every Greek root numbered G1 ἄλφα → G5624). Terena Gill annotated δύναμις G1411 in June 2026.
- Pem's line, « rêver le monde et vivre le monde rêvé », is Homer's pair **ὄναρ / ὕπαρ** (dream in sleep / waking
  vision; *Odyssey* 19, « οὐκ ὄναρ, ἀλλ' ὕπαρ »). ὄναρ is G3677 (six times, all in Matthew).
- Bachelard's elements already have series: fire = Purinos, air = ἀβαρής, earth / tree = δένδρον, Oikeios. **Water is
  missing** — the island series can be it.

## Per application
- **Therapeia (Paxos)** — `cases/2027_therapeia.md`: title proposal **ὄναρ / ὕπαρ** (beside « Paxos, l'île rêvée »);
  water, the missing element; continue ἀβαρής; δένδρον for the olive trees and cypresses. In Greece, one line on the
  Strong's numbering makes the Greek names readable.
- **Paréidolie 2027 (via a gallery)** — `cases/2027_pareidolie.md`: the drawing-series proposal starts from the two
  largest series, ἀβαρής (262) and στρέφω (184, 26 years of practice); each title with its Greek word and meaning.
- **DRAC AIC / Récits de Paysages** — `cases/2027_drac.md`: the project as Bachelard's elements already begun — fire
  (Purinos), air (ἀβαρής), earth (δένδρον, Oikeios) — and the water still to make; Oikeios (place, belonging) for a
  landscape residency.
- **CoG 2027 (Niels Bohr Institute / Max Planck) and Arts at CERN** — `CALENDAR.md`: δύναμις, power and force; works
  *Strong force* (the strong interaction is CERN's field), *Géodésique*, *Black hole door / Gamma / Flesh*, *Möbius*.
  The « CoG 26 » selection (13) was the 2026 application: check what it shares with δύναμις.
- **Salon de Montrouge 2028**: the « Montrouge » selection (100) is the 2027 dossier to start from.
