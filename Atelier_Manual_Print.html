Atelier Studio Manual: Data & Operations

This document is a beginner's guide to managing the Atelier PEM Hub. Whether you are an art assistant or a data administrator, follow these steps to keep the studio running with high precision.

Part 1: Tab-by-Tab Orientation

01. Overview 📊

Purpose: The "Health Check."

Action: Use this to see how many works were created this year and what techniques are currently most frequent. No data input happens here; it's a mirror of the rest of the system.

02. Inventory 🖼️

Purpose: The Master Record.

Action: Every single physical work must be listed here.

Rule: If it exists in the studio, it must have an "OeuvreID" and a status of "Studio".

03. Constellation 🌌

Purpose: Artistic Curation.

Action: A visual map of the works. Use the Lasso tool (click and drag) to select multiple works for a PDF checklist or a private gallery link.

04. Production (Kanban) 🏗️

Purpose: The "Factory Floor."

Action: Drag artwork cards from "Idée" to "En cours" to "Séchage".

Rule: Never skip the "Séchage" (Drying) stage for oil paintings—it signals to the team that the work cannot be moved yet.

05. Logistics 📦

Purpose: Shipping & Loans.

Action: Record every time a work leaves the studio (e.g., to a gallery or a photographer). Input the destination contact and the "Scheduled Date."

06. Vault 🔒

Purpose: Legal & Certificates.

Action: Upload PDF versions of Authenticity Certificates, Invoices, and Insurance contracts here.

Rule: Link every document to its specific "OeuvreID".

07. Contacts & World Map 👥

Purpose: The Rolodex.

Action: Enter new collectors, suppliers, and galleries.

Rule: Ensure every contact has a City and Country so they appear correctly on the World Map.

08. Pipeline ⏳

Purpose: Deadlines.

Action: Check this daily for upcoming exhibition deadlines or "To-Do" reminders.

09. Fiscal 💸

Purpose: Money.

Action: Input all receipts for supplies.

Rule: The "Date" field must match the receipt date exactly to ensure the tax reports are accurate.

10. Concepts 💡

Purpose: Ideas.

Action: Log "Burning Ideas" here. If an idea becomes a real painting, link it to the new Inventory record.

11. Stock & Stock-take 🧪

Purpose: Studio Supplies.

Action: Perform a physical count of pigments and canvases on the last Friday of the month. Enter the numbers in "Stock-take."

12. System ⚙️

Purpose: The Suggestion Box.

Action: If the software is confusing or needs a new feature, log a Suggestion here.

Part 2: Data Entry Best Practices

  • Capitalization: Use Title Case for everything. "CoG 2026", not "cog 2026".
  • Dimensions: Always "Height × Width" in cm. Use the "×" sign.
  • Images: Use AVIF format. If a work has no photo, turn the "Needs Photograph" flag ON.
  • No Gaps: A database is only useful if it's complete. If a field is empty, the system loses leverage.
