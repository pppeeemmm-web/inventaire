## What the interview found

1. **The pattern is « built, then redesigned from scratch »**, not « built and unused ». The AI desk is the example: the purpose was right (one place for every AI engine), but the design was quick, nothing was tested end to end, and the copy and buttons were strange. Cause: building from a short request without the full picture.
2. **The fix Pem asks for is advisory**: before building, the assistant writes the consequences, scope, pluses and minuses and places the request in the whole system. That is the lifecycle rule Pem picked; it goes with « design first » (STRATEGY 2026-11-14).
3. **The build phase has an end.** Pem wants to wind down the Claude subscription once everything is ready and tested. Every new idea therefore competes with finishing: what is half-built gets finished and tested before anything new starts.
4. **Speed and flow beat features.** Loading times, catalogue reloads between tabs and container-in-container layouts cost Pem more than any missing feature.
5. **Commercial stays last, for a new reason**: no case within two months; when it comes it is a learning exercise and a way to stay independent. This keeps the STRATEGY ranking.

## Assistant's view — the full picture Pem asked for

Four weeks to 2026-12-17 is about two batches. Five « Now » items do not fit as five separate builds, so:

1. **One window, two uses.** Quick capture and the debug window are the same thing: a small window that stays on top (desk hotkey, phone shortcut) taking text, a voice note, a photo or a screenshot. A switch says « idea / bug ». Both land in one inbox; bug items become reports for Claude. One build instead of two, and one layer instead of two.
2. **Speed is the Journal's design.** The Journal flow and « no catalogue reload between tabs » are one problem: what loads, when, and what stays cached on the device. Measure first, then design the Journal around what loads fast.
3. **The AI desk: fix or freeze.** With four weeks left, either redesign and test it end to end in one batch, or hide what does not work. Shipping it half-fixed again is the pattern to stop.
4. **Freeze before the wind-down.** From about 2026-12-10, nothing new: only tests on the Docker copy, fixes, and a runbook so the app runs without Claude (deploy, backups, what to do when something breaks).
5. **Commercial won't make it before the wind-down.** No case within two months and still last means it gets built after 2026-12-17, by Pem as the learning exercise — or with a short subscription restart when a case comes. Its design note can still be written now, at no build cost.
6. **Later items stay usable without new code.** Making links in Constellation (Pem's plan) needs no build; the digests and 3D wait.
