The Studio Bible

The Comprehensive Operating Manual for the PEM Hub Infrastructure.

01. The Grand Architecture

The Hub is split into four distinct "Surfaces," each serving a specific audience. Access is controlled by roles and secret tokens.

A. The Atelier (Internal)

The nerve center. Used for inventory, sales, and logistics. This is where 99% of administrative work happens.

B. Collectionneurs (Private)

A gated portal for VIP clients. They see only the works you have specifically shared with them.

C. Galeries (Partner)

A collaborative surface for partners. They can view consignments and manage shared checklists.

D. Portfolio (Public)

The external face of the studio. No prices, no private data—just the art and the bio.

02. The Atelier Portal: 16 Tabs of Power

Once you click "ENTRER" on the Atelier tile, you have access to these specific modules:

Overview (Tableau de Bord)

What it does: Displays "Live Intelligence"—counts of works created this year, technique breakdowns, and urgent pipeline deadlines.

Hidden Feature: The "Burning Concepts" list shows ideas with high "Energy" ratings that need attention.

Inventory (Inventaire)

What it does: The master list of every artwork ever made.

How to use: Use the search bar to find works by Title or ID. Click a row to open the Work Drawer on the right.

The 80/80 Rule: Double-click any thumbnail image to expand the drawer to 80% of the screen for high-detail inspection.

Constellation

What it does: A visual map where X = Time and Y = Technique.

The Lasso: Hold the mouse and drag a square over the nodes to select them. This is how you "curate" a selection for an exhibition or a PDF.

Production (Kanban)

What it does: Tracks works in progress. Drag cards from Idée to Encadrement.

Workflow: When a work hits the "Photographié" column, ensure the high-res image is uploaded to the Vault.

Logistics & Shipments

What it does: Tracks the physical location of works.

How to use: Create a "New Shipment" whenever art leaves the studio. This automatically updates the work's location in the inventory.

Vault (Le Coffre)

What it does: Digital document storage.

Action: Upload Certificates of Authenticity and Insurance docs here. They are permanently linked to the Work ID.

03. Core Operational Workflows

Curation & Exports (The "Leverage" Flow)

  1. Selection: Choose works in the Inventory (via checkboxes) or Constellation (via Lasso).
  2. The Dock: A black bar will appear at the bottom showing N works selected.
  3. Export PDF Checklist: Click this to generate a museum-standard PDF with thumbnails and prices.
  4. Create Private Link: Generates a secret URL. Send this to a collector. It allows them to view only the selected works in a premium interface.

Fiscal & Expense Management

  1. Navigate to the Fiscal tab.
  2. Click Add Expense.
  3. Input the exact amount, the Supplier (linked from Contacts), and the Receipt Date.
  4. The system automatically calculates the total burn rate per month.

Stock & Material Audit

To prevent running out of pigments or canvas, a physical audit is required monthly.

The Audit Rule: Go to the Stock-take tab. Enter the physical count. If the system says you have 10 and you count 5, the system will highlight the discrepancy in red. Log this in the System Ledger.

04. The Data Standard (Non-Negotiable)

The system is only as good as the data entered. Follow these rules strictly:

Typography

  • Capitalize proper nouns: Oil on Canvas, not oil on canvas.
  • Dimensions: Use × (multiplication), not x (the letter).
  • Metric: cm only.

Imagery

  • Format: .avif (Primary) or .webp.
  • Resolution: Minimum 2000px on the long edge.
  • Flag: Use the Needs Photo toggle if the image is missing.

05. System Maintenance & Evolution

If the system breaks or you have an idea for a new feature:

  1. Go to the System tab (top right of the Atelier).
  2. Select 💡 Suggestion or 🔧 Maintenance.
  3. Type the details. The developers check this log daily to improve the site.
