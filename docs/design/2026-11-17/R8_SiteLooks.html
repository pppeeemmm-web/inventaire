Three looks for thepemworkshop.com

Real works from the catalogue, shown three ways. Each row is the first screen a visitor sees on a computer and on a phone. The fourth row is one work's own page, the same whichever look you pick: there the painting appears as it is, with its facts and an « Enquire » button.

The site has two jobs. The stage (landing, sequences) can be a creative work with light, sky and motion. The reference (one page per work, link previews, search) shows the painting plainly. The three looks are three possible stages, and you can mix them.

The work as it is: no tint, haze or gloss. Detail shots, facts, availability.

Mixing is allowed

For example: Détail on the landing (the first impression, moving), Plein cadre when someone browses the works, Scène kept as an « Accrochage » view for the room and the sky, and the plain work page behind every image.

What has to be chosen first is only the landing and the default view of the works. The rest can stay as it is until you decide.

Mockups only. Images are the catalogue's real files; the medium (« Huile sur toile »), series and « Disponible » shown here are examples, not catalogue data. Name shown as « Pierre Emmanuel Moulin » / « Pem » as a placeholder for the one-name decision.
