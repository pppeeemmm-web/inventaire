## The idea
Homer: **ὄναρ**, the dream seen in sleep, and **ὕπαρ**, the waking vision that comes true. The film moves from one to
the other: darkness and blur → a hand draws → the drawing becomes the world outside → waking. Pem's line closes it:
« rêver le monde et vivre le monde rêvé ».
**Distance and mystery:** no face, no studio, no voice explaining. Only hands, paper, light, sky, and the works. The
viewer sees *how*, not *who*.

## What Pem films (shot list, one phone, natural light)
No studio needed: any table near a window does.
1. **Hands, blank paper, first line**: from above, graphite or pierre noire; 3–4 takes of 15 s.
2. **Macro of the line**: phone 2–3 cm from the paper, light from the side so the grain shows.
3. **Smudge**: a finger or stump softens a line until it floats (ἀβαρής, « weightless »).
4. **Window light, out of focus**: curtain or shutter at dawn or dusk; tap to focus on nothing.
5. **Sky at dusk**: phone still on a support, 20 s, wind in trees (pines or cypresses if possible).
6. **Black**: lens covered for 3 s, with the pencil sound recorded separately (phone close to the paper).
Rules: lock exposure and focus (long press), no zoom, slow or no movement, 10 s minimum per take.
